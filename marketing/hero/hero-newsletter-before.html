Maple Grove Elementary: October Newsletter

From: Principal's Office (sample) · To: Maple Grove Families

Dear Maple Grove Families, what a wonderful start to the year it has been! Our students have settled into their routines, the library is buzzing with new books, and we have been so grateful for the volunteers who helped with the beautification project over the long weekend.

From the Principal's Desk

As the weather cools, please remember that students go outside for recess unless it is raining, so jackets are a good idea. Lost and found is overflowing near the gym, and anything left by the end of the month will be donated. Please also remember to update your contact information in the family portal if anything has changed over the summer, and to review the updated pick-up line procedures sent home in September.

Upcoming Happenings

We have a busy few weeks ahead. Picture day is coming up on Thursday, October 22, and will take place in the gym from 8:30 to 11:30 in the morning; order forms will go home the week before, and retakes will be offered in the spring. Please do not wait until the last minute to send in your forms, as the photographer cannot guarantee a spot for late orders.

Our PTA is thrilled to announce the return of the Harvest Carnival on Friday, October 30, from 5:30 to 8:00 pm on the playground. There will be games, a cake walk, and a chili supper, and costumes are welcome (no masks that cover the whole face, please). We are still looking for volunteers to run booths, and sign-up sheets are available in the front office, so if you can spare an hour we would love your help.

Curriculum Corner

Third grade is wrapping up its unit on local habitats, fourth grade has begun fractions, and our kindergarteners have learned all about apples. Reading logs are due at the end of each week, and we encourage families to read together for at least twenty minutes each night. Math homework packets continue to go home on Mondays.

Conferences and Schedule Changes

Parent-teacher conferences will be held on Tuesday, November 10, between 3:00 and 7:00 pm in your child's classroom. Conferences are fifteen minutes long, and a sign-up link will arrive by email next week. Please also note that on Wednesday, November 18, we will have an early release day so teachers can plan together, and students will be dismissed at 12:30 pm. Aftercare will be available as usual for registered families.

Reminders

Please send a water bottle with your child each day, label sweaters and lunch boxes, and make sure that all medications are dropped off in the front office with the proper forms. The cafeteria menu for October is attached, and the lunch account balance can be checked online. Thank you, as always, for being such a caring community.
