Upcoming Happenings

We have a busy few weeks ahead, so here is what is coming up. Picture day is next Thursday, Oct 22, and will take place in the gym from 8:30am to 11:30am. Individual portraits will be taken by grade, and order forms will go home with students the week before.

Our PTA is thrilled to announce the return of the Harvest Carnival on Friday, Oct 30, from 5:30pm to 8:00pm on the playground. There will be games, a cake walk and a chili supper, and costumes are welcome (no masks that cover the whole face, please).
