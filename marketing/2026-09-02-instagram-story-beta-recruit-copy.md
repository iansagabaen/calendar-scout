# Instagram Story — beta-tester recruit copy

Goal: get a few more friends (parents, drowning in school newsletters) to try
Calendar Scout and report back. Should read like Ian talking, not like a launch.

All five are deliberately **short** — a story is glanceable, not a paragraph.
Each fits one 9:16 frame with room to spare (verified in `instagram-story-preview.html`).

**Core promise every option keeps truthful:**

- Forward one real school newsletter / event-packed email to **scout@sendtoschedule.com**
- It emails back the events it found, each with a one-tap "add to calendar" link
- Free, no account, experimental, made by one person (Ian), still misses things
- Doesn't store the email you send it
- The ask: forward **one** real email, then say **what it got wrong**
- Always a low-pressure out ("no worries if not", "only if you get these")

No fake urgency. No "limited spots". No "link in bio". No emoji-as-marketing.

> Address note: the landing page currently shows `forward@sendtoschedule.com`, but
> any prefix `@sendtoschedule.com` reaches the Scout (per the site FAQ), so
> `scout@sendtoschedule.com` is used here because it reads better. Swap to `forward@`
> in all five if Ian wants to match the site exactly.

---

## Option 1 — The honest builder

> i built a tiny email tool and need friends to try and break it: forward one real school newsletter to scout@sendtoschedule.com, then tell me what came back wrong. total no-worries if not.

**Why it works:** "try and break it" asks for criticism, not applause; lands with
friends who like being early on a thing.
**Risk:** none really — just don't pair it the same day with another "it's rough / just me" line or it becomes a tic.

---

## Option 2 — The specific pain

> that school newsletter with picture day buried in paragraph nine — forward one to scout@sendtoschedule.com and it sends back calendar links. tell me what it missed; ignore this if you don't get these.

**Why it works:** "paragraph nine" is instantly familiar to the target parent and
self-selects everyone else out.
**Risk:** if the dash opener reads as a hook, make it "you know the school newsletter with picture day buried in paragraph nine?"

---

## Option 3 — The blunt one-liner

> free thing i made: forward a school newsletter to scout@sendtoschedule.com, get calendar links back, tell me if it's garbage. no worries if not.

**Why it works:** nothing to skim; people who already trust Ian just need the
address and the ask. Good as a text-only slide over his face or handwriting.
**Risk:** so terse it can read as a broadcast — keep it visually personal so it doesn't feel forwarded.

---

## Option 4 — The favor

> small favor for the parents here — forward one real school email to scout@sendtoschedule.com and text me what broke. two minutes, and totally fine to skip.

**Why it works:** "favor" + a bounded two-minute cost is easier to say yes to than
"try my product."
**Risk:** keep the estimate honest — the real task is ~2 minutes, so "two minutes" is safe; don't pad it.

---

## Option 5 — Origin in one line

> i lose an afternoon to school newsletters every fall, so i built a tool: forward one to scout@sendtoschedule.com and tell me what it gets wrong. only if you actually get these.

**Why it works:** the "lose an afternoon every fall" beat is shared experience —
reads as venting, not pitching — and the ask rides along in the same breath.
**Risk:** none major; if it feels a hair long on his phone, drop "every fall".

---

## How to use these

**Sticker:** plain text slide — dark text on a plain light background (or Ian's own
photo dimmed behind it), no design. Add only a **"DM me"** / question sticker or the
line "reply to this" so answers land in DMs, not public reactions. Type
`scout@sendtoschedule.com` **as text**, never a link sticker — a link sticker is what
makes a story read as an ad. A link to sendtoschedule.com only ever goes on a
*second* slide labeled plainly ("more about it here"), never as the main CTA.

**When:** weekday ~7:30–9:00am (phones after drop-off), or Sunday ~7–9pm (looking at
the week ahead). Avoid Friday/Saturday night.

**Follow-up DM for anyone who replies** (paste as-is):

> really appreciate it — forward whichever school email is bugging you most to scout@sendtoschedule.com and you'll get calendar links back in a few seconds.
> then just tell me anything it missed or got wrong, even tiny stuff. no rush.
